Monthly QA trend

QA rows grouped by the month the conversation happened, joined to the conversation log's timestamps. Reviewed counts rows graded Pass, Partial pass, Fail, or System Update; pass rate is Pass ÷ (Pass + Partial + Fail). Apr* covers Apr 27–30 only and Aug* runs through Aug 10. Deflection is the share of logged conversations with the log's deflected flag — this flag basis runs higher than the headline no-handoff deflection.
Pass rate has held in the low-to-mid 50s since May, with July at 57.8%; August's 40.8% is a partial month with only 49 reviewed rows. Flag-based deflection has held around 90% since May.

Repeat contact after deflection

Post-widget same-issue ticket rate from the Widget Repeat Contact tab: confirmed cases where the same customer opened their own support ticket within 7 days of a widget conversation that was never escalated to a human, over all never-escalated conversations that month. Lower is better — treat as a containment-quality signal, not a definitive deflection-failure rate. Evidence rows are de-identified on this page; customer names live in the sheet. Refreshed Aug 14, 2026.
10.0% of July's 180 fully-contained conversations came back as a same-issue support ticket within 7 days — up 3.8pp from June's 6.2% baseline. Every one is a conversation the headline deflection rate counts as a win.

Data status — —

What is loaded for the reporting month and what is still pending. Built from the data itself, so it updates each month without editing.
How to refresh this dashboard each month
  1. KB Scorecard. Append one value, in the same position, to momMonths and to every array in momSeries, roleSeries and searchSeries. Then set reportingMonth (current, prior, abbreviations, year, as-of date). The KB headline, support impact, search & findability, segments, KB success, story cards and KB commentary are all derived, so nothing else is retyped.
  2. KB traffic (Amplitude). Add the month to KB_TRAFFIC (months, views, visitors, guideViews).
  3. Ticket volume. From the Non-Proactive export, append the month to ticketGroupMonths and one value to every group in ticketGroups. This unlocks the overall deflection share, KB success rate, capacity signal and the ticket-volume sections.
  4. Widget. Add the month to widget.byMonth and append to widget.monthly. Add its rawSignals. Mark it in firstRead until the final pull and QA are done, then remove it. Append to the capacity signal and repeat-contact tracker once tickets and validation are loaded.
  5. Autoresponder. Only when the month is confirmed as Support only: append it to supportFunnel, set funnelMonth, and remove the month from email.unconfirmed.
  6. QA reviews. Replace the widget and email handoff-reason and KB-update blocks and set each block's month. Their section labels clear on their own. Rewrite any commentary that is flagged "Written for <older month>" and update its data-written.
  7. Single-month sections. When one is rebuilt, update its entry in state.sectionMonths. Use state.dataNotes for anything a reader should know that the data can't say (a mismatch, a blocked source), and uncheckedRise for a KB move that still needs checking.
  8. Check. The header should read "monthly data checks passed", and the Data status panel above shows what is loaded for the month. Month keys are Jan to Dec; after December use Jan '27.

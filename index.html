Capacity signal — self-service share of demand

Built because a minutes-per-touch conversion isn't available yet from Support leadership, so touches removed can't be turned into hours or headcount. This tracks capacity impact a different way: what share of total demand (tickets plus widget conversations without a handoff) never reached a person. It can rise simply because ticket volume falls, so read it alongside the handoff rate. iContained = widget conversations minus the ones handed off (so a conversation only counts if it had no handoff, no double-counting). Tickets = all-category Non-Proactive ticket volume, Ticket ID deduped. Demand = tickets + contained.

Repeat contact after containment

Same customer, same issue, own support ticket within 7 days of a fully-contained widget conversation. Lower is better — a containment-quality signal, not a failure rate for No-Handoff Containment.
2.8% of August's contained conversations came back as same-issue tickets within 7 days — down sharply from 8.5% in July — each one is counted as a win by the headline No-Handoff Containment metric.
